Life Library privacy policy

Effective date: 10 September 2026.

Life Library is a private tool used solely by its owner to access, organize, upload, download and verify their own Google Drive files, including files uploaded through Google's interface. It is not offered to other users.

Data and access

The application uses Google Drive file content and metadata only for the owner's requested file management. Its Drive authorization permits reading and managing files; normal operations are limited to the designated library folders. File deletion and sharing are not automated.

Storage and disclosure

Formal files remain in the owner's Google Drive. Selected working copies and verification records may be stored on the owner's computer. Credentials are kept in the owner's 1Password infrastructure vault and protected local runtime configuration. Existing archive copies may remain in the owner's other storage. This documentation site receives no Drive files or OAuth tokens. It contains no analytics or advertising scripts.

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or used to develop or train generalized AI or machine learning models. Any AI assistance is performed only as requested by the owner through their chosen tools and accounts. Hosting providers may process ordinary connection logs under their own policies.

Control and retention

The owner controls retention and can revoke Life Library's access in Google Account security settings. Revoking access stops future API access but does not delete files or existing backups. Files remain until the owner chooses to remove them.

Life Library's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including Limited Use requirements.

For questions, use this project's issue tracker; do not post personal files or credentials.
